Data protection policy

Three rules cover most of your day.

1. Look at the label. Every mail, file and channel carries one. It tells you who may see it and whether it may leave the ministry.

2. Share from Ortus, not around it. Send a link instead of an attachment. Do not move work files to private mail or private phones.

3. Say it early. If something went to the wrong person, tell the service desk within the hour. Nobody is punished for reporting.
